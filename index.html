Privacy

Our commitment to your data

Privacy isn't a feature — it's a foundation. Here's exactly how we handle your data across all CK Creative apps.

Data We Collect

We collect only what's essential to operate the app — nothing more. For Local Journal, your entries are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Data We Never Sell

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is not our product.

Your Control

You can delete your data at any time by uninstalling the app. For questions about your data or to request deletion, contact us directly at the email below.

For app-specific privacy policies, contact us at ckcreativeandconsulting@gmail.com. We will respond within 2 business days.
